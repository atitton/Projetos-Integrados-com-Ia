# Cena turma (wall ball) — prompt v2 para o Veo (Kie.ai)

Objetivo: manter o clima da versão 1 (turma no box, luz quente), mas com o movimento
real do wall ball: agacha com a bola, sobe, arremessa, a bola bate no alvo da parede,
volta e é pega, e a pessoa já desce no próximo agachamento.

## Prompt (em inglês, o Veo segue melhor)

Realistic handheld documentary footage inside a small industrial functional-fitness gym,
warm morning light through a roll-up door. Medium-wide shot, camera at chest height,
slowly tracking sideways. In sharp focus in the foreground: one athlete in a black t-shirt
doing continuous wall ball reps facing a plain concrete wall with a round orange target
painted about 3 meters high. Each rep follows real physics: holding a soft 6 kg medicine
ball at the chest, she squats deep, drives up explosively, and throws the ball upward out
of her hands; the ball flies in a clear arc, visibly hits the target on the wall with a
small bounce, falls back down, and she catches it at chest height and flows straight into
the next squat. The ball always stays in front of the wall, bouncing off its surface.
Behind her, three other athletes slightly out of focus do the same movement in rhythm at
their own wall targets. Natural sweat, chalk dust in the light, no text, no signs, no logos,
no posters. 16:9, 8 seconds, 24 fps, cinematic but natural color.

## Negative prompt (se a ferramenta aceitar)

ball passing through wall, ball merging with wall, ball disappearing, ball stuck to hands,
holding the ball overhead without throwing, extra balls, extra limbs, distorted hands,
text, letters, signage, posters, logos, watermark

## Dicas
- Gere 2 variações e escolha a melhor; o Veo erra física de bola com frequência.
- Se sobrar um erro curto, me mande assim mesmo: no vídeo final eu uso só o trecho bom
  (a cena precisa de ~6 s; hoje o clipe é esticado para 12 s em câmera lenta).
- Salve como `cena-turma.mp4`, substituindo o anterior.
